    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How is this different from voicemail or an answering service?

    Voicemail waits for the caller to leave a message, and many don't. An answering service takes a message for you. GoSnagcall texts the caller back within 60 seconds, keeps the conversation going and, on Growth and Pro, finds out what the job is before you call back. It's a flat monthly price, not per call or per minute.

    How fast can I get set up?

    New accounts are onboarded through a waitlist. After you reserve your spot, we let you know your start date, based on the businesses registered ahead of you. From that date, getting your business number approved for texting typically takes 10–14 days. You pay for your first month when you reserve, but that month only starts counting once your line goes live, so you never pay for time spent waiting.

    Do I need to be good with technology?

    No. We set everything up for you, including the carrier registration and your text templates. You fill out a short form and answer a few questions by text or email.

    Is my customers' data safe?

    Yes. Conversations, contact details and appointments are stored on servers we run ourselves in the EU, protected under the GDPR, one of the strictest privacy laws in the world. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS), only our team can access the data to run your service, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. Text messages themselves are delivered through our SMS provider and U.S. carriers, like every business text.

    Which AI do you use? Do messages go to ChatGPT?

    No. GoSnagcall runs its own AI language models on our own servers. Your customers' messages are never sent to ChatGPT, OpenAI or any other outside AI service, and we don't use your data to train AI models for anyone else.

    What if it doesn't work out for my business?

    You're covered by our 14-day guarantee: if GoSnagcall isn't a fit, you get a full refund within 14 days of your go-live date, not from sign-up or payment.

    Is there a long-term contract?

    No. Every plan is month-to-month. Cancel anytime, no cancellation fees.

    Do you offer yearly billing?

    Yes. Pay yearly and get 2 months free. You can start monthly and switch to yearly at any time. The 14-day money-back guarantee from your go-live date applies to yearly plans too.

    What happens if I go over 30 missed calls on Starter?

    We'll give you a heads-up by text and email when you reach 80% of your monthly limit. Once you hit 30, new missed calls won't get a text-back until your next month starts, or you can upgrade to Growth anytime for unlimited text-backs.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time as your business needs change.

    What industries do you work with?

    GoSnagcall is built for home and professional service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, garage door repair, dental, veterinary, chiropractic, med spas and similar trades.

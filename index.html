Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get set up?

New accounts are onboarded through a short waitlist — getting your business number approved for messaging typically takes 10–14 days. You reserve your spot by paying for your first month upfront, but that month only starts counting once your line actually goes live, so you never pay for time spent waiting.

What if it doesn't work out for my business?

You're covered by our 14-day guarantee: if GoSnagcall isn't a fit, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your go-live date — not from sign-up or payment.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Every plan is month-to-month — cancel anytime, no cancellation fees.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade at any time as your business needs change.

What industries do you work with?

GoSnagcall is built for home and professional service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control, garage door repair, dental, veterinary, chiropractic, med spas, and similar trades.
